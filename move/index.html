05 What Will Should Be Pushing For
The Real Ask — In Priority Order
  1. Lease termination paid directly. $4,290 is a documented, verifiable, unavoidable penalty that exists solely because you're accepting their offer. If the company pays it outside the lump sum, your entire $6,728 net goes toward the actual move. This is the highest-leverage ask — frame it to Will as a cost-reimbursement, not a lifestyle perk.
  2. Clarify whether the $10K ask is approved or still being negotiated. If Will's pushing for $10K and they counter at $5K, the math on this specific move makes $5K effectively symbolic — it covers the lease fee and nothing else after tax.
  3. Timing of disbursement. Lump sum paid while you're a TX resident = $307 more in your pocket. Ask whether it can be processed with your final Austin paycheck.
  4. Clawback terms. Most relocation agreements require pro-rated repayment if you leave within 1–2 years. Get this in writing before signing — especially important since you're targeting this as a stepping-stone role.
  5. The industry benchmark context for Will. Average renter relocation packages run $14,600–$21,800. $10K gross at before-tax is below midpoint. For a Sr. Ops Manager hire at $110K, $15K gross or a managed relocation covering movers + car transport directly is not unreasonable. Will should know this number before he walks into that conversation.
