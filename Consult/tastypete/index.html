Backyard Roots

We started as a backyard pit in Central Texas, where the only customers were the neighbors and the smoke. Our story is one of tradition, slow-smoked turkey legs and brisket made with love, and a commitment to the craft that has grown by word of mouth.

The Menu

Smoked Turkey Legs

Brined, smoked low and slow, and served hot off the smoker. Each leg is a piece of heritage, seasoned with our signature rub and smoked for 12+ hours.

Texas-Style Brisket

Long smoke, proper rest, and sliced fresh to order. Our brisket is smoked for 12+ hours, then rested for 12 hours before being sliced and served hot.
