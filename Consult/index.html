How this actually goes

Five steps, and you can stop after any of them.

Nothing gets built without your say-so, and nothing costs you anything until step three.

1

I look up what's already out there

I find whatever exists about your business online — Google listing, Facebook, reviews, a logo if there is one — and use it to put together a bare-bones starting structure.

2

I build a free preview on Wix

A real, working site laid out around your business — built from what I found, with placeholders standing in for anything I don't have yet. You get a link to look at it whenever you want.

3

You choose a path, or you don't

If it's not for you, no hard feelings and no charge. If it is, we pick one-time build or managed monthly and I get it live.

4

You send me the real details

Once you're in, you hand off anything you want in the finished site — photos, exact copy, corrections, whatever's missing — and I swap it in for the placeholders.

5

We make sure people can find it

I set up or clean up your Google Business Profile — that's the part that actually gets you found on Google. Social media isn't something I handle; I'll say so upfront rather than pretend otherwise.

Transparency, not fine print
