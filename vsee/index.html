FAQ

Straight answers, Valley edition.

Which box should I get?
For most people, the Elite Mini. It's the best buy in the lineup. If you watch movies and shows all day and want the best hardware, get the Elite Ultra. The V6 Max is the entry box, and the V6 Plus is the step up if you want to watch two things at once.
Where do we meet?
At one of three public spots: east Bethlehem for Allentown and Bethlehem, downtown Easton for Easton and Phillipsburg, and Phillipsburg for Clinton Township and the rest of the NJ side. I text you the exact spot once we set a time.
How fast can I get one?
Usually same day. Sometimes it's the next day if our timing doesn't line up.
I'm west of Allentown.
For one or a few boxes, meet me at the east Bethlehem spot. If you're buying a full case for resale, I'll come to you.
What about Philadelphia or farther?
I ship it. $15 for the first box, $3 for each additional box in the same order, paid by Zelle before it ships.
What does this actually replace on my bill?
The TV half. Whether your bill says Service Electric, Astound (the old RCN), or Xfinity, you keep their internet and drop the TV package and box rentals. That's the expensive half. The calculator at the top shows what it's costing you.
So I still need internet?
Yes. The box streams over your internet, so you keep the one service that's actually useful and stop renting television from the same company.
Is there really no monthly fee?
Really. One payment, cash or Zelle. No subscription, no rental, no equipment fee.
Can one box run more than one TV?
No, it's one box per TV. Bedroom and living room means two boxes.
Does it work on a phone or tablet?
No. It's a box that plugs into your TV over HDMI, not a phone app.
How far behind live is Heat Live?
Roughly 30 to 120 seconds behind real time, about the same as most internet live TV. Mute the group chat until the play's over.
What's the Elite Ultra app install?
The Elite Ultra ships with just the Heat apps. For $50, I install everything else before pickup so it's ready when you plug it in. Because I open it to do that, the box won't be factory-sealed. The other three boxes already come with everything installed.
Do you sell to resellers?
Yes. The V6 Max comes by the case: 20 boxes for $3,800. Text me to order.
What if it acts up after I get home?
Text me. Support is a text thread with the person who sold it to you.
