The usual way

Getting a website shouldn't feel like buying a used car.

Most small businesses get burned the same three ways. I built this service to be the opposite of all three.

Pain #1

The $4,000 quote

Agencies price for companies with marketing departments. You're an owner-operator. You need a working website, not a discovery phase, a brand workshop, and a 12-week timeline.

Pain #2

The hostage situation

Some "web guys" register your domain in their name and hold the only login. Try to leave, change anything, or even see what you're paying for — good luck.

Pain #3

The ghost

Launch day comes, the invoice clears, and then… silence. A phone number changes, hours change, and updating your own site becomes a support ticket nobody answers.

Here's the whole process, in five steps. You can stop after any of them — and nothing costs you a dime until step three.

How this actually goes

Five steps. Zero risk until you say go.

No sales calls, no proposals, no "let me check with my team." Just a straight line from "I need a site" to "customers can find me."

  1. 01

    I look up what's already out there Free

    I find whatever exists about your business online — your Google listing, Facebook page, reviews, a logo if there is one — and use it to sketch a starting structure. You do nothing.

  2. 02

    I design your free preview Free

    A real, working homepage built around your business, with placeholders standing in for anything I don't have yet. Usually ready within 1 business day. You get a link — look at it whenever you want, share it with your partner.

  3. 03

    You choose a path — or you don't

    If it's not for you, no hard feelings and no charge. If it is, we pick the one-time build or the managed monthly plan, and I build it on your own account.

  4. 04

    You send me the real details

    Once you're in, hand off anything you want in the finished site — photos, exact wording, corrections, whatever's missing — and I swap it in for the placeholders.

  5. 05

    We make sure people can find it

    I set up or clean up your Google Business Profile — hours, photos, categories, directions in one tap. That's the part that actually puts you in front of nearby customers.

Nothing gets built without your say-so. The preview is step two and it costs nothing — you don't spend a dollar until you've seen the actual site and decided it's worth it.
