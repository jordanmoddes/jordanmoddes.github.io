04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Is the first preview really free?

Yes. The first concept is a way to see whether the direction fits your business before you commit to the build.

What do I need to provide?

A short explanation of what you do, the area you serve, your preferred contact method, and any real photos, reviews, or brand materials you already have. If your wording is rough, that is fine — shaping it is part of the work.

What if I already own a domain?

That is useful. Your existing domain can be connected rather than replaced. You stay the owner of the account and domain.

Is Google Business Profile included?

Yes, setup is included with every build. The managed plan also includes ongoing profile upkeep.

What counts as a monthly edit?

A monthly edit is a focused update such as changing hours, swapping a photo, updating a service, or revising a section of copy. Larger new pages or redesigns would be scoped separately before work begins.
